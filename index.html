Who we are

A sky full of builders

Celestius is the student-run engineering and innovation community of CIT Chennai. We bring together developers, designers, speakers, editors and organisers who love turning ideas into real things.

From flagship hackathons to hands-on workshops, we learn by building, and we build together. Like stars in a constellation, every member adds to the bigger picture.
